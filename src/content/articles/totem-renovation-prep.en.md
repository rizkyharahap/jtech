Totem renovation touches both the visual identity of a fuel station and its supporting
structure. A few things determine whether the work runs smoothly or disrupts operations.

## Before work starts

**Survey the existing condition.** A foundation pole that looks sound from outside may be
corroded within. This decides whether it is enough to replace panels or whether the pole
itself must go.

**Set the working window.** Work in the service area should be scheduled during low
operational hours. The totem is also the entry marker, so visibility matters.

**Prepare access routes.** Space for lifting equipment and a safe work area are frequently
overlooked in planning.

## During the work

Daily coordination with facility management allows fast decisions, especially when site
conditions differ from the drawings.

## After completion

Before-and-after documentation is not just a report annex — it makes the next periodic
inspection far easier.
