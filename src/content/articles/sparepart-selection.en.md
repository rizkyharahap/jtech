The wrong spare part rarely shows immediately. It surfaces later as repeat failure or a
volume difference that is hard to explain.

## Three things to match

**Physical compatibility.** Connection dimensions, thread and mounting position. A part that
looks "similar" often differs by a few millimetres at the decisive point.

**Technical suitability.** Pressure range, flow rate and seal material must suit the
application. The wrong material may work initially, then fail far sooner.

**Unit identification.** Dispenser serial and model numbers are the most reliable way to
match a part. A photo of the component to be replaced helps a great deal.

## Information worth preparing

- Dispenser brand and model
- Photo or part code of the old component
- Quantity required
- Urgency of replacement

With that information we can check availability and compatibility before quoting — so the
unit does not have to be opened twice.
