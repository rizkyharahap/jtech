The safety of fuel-station installations and equipment is governed by official regulation.
This article summarises the general principles and links to official sources — it is not a
legal interpretation.

## Core principles

**Ignition source control.** Filling and storage areas have different requirements for
electrical equipment, hot work and the materials permitted in each zone.

**System integrity.** Connections, valves and piping runs must be kept to specification so
they do not become leak points.

**Periodic inspection and testing.** Oil and gas installations and equipment carry inspection
and testing obligations; how these are performed follows the applicable guidance.

**Personnel competence.** Work on certain installations and equipment requires competent
and, in defined circumstances, certified personnel.

## How to use this information

This page is an introduction. For technical and compliance decisions, refer directly to the
official guidance linked below and consult the relevant authority.
