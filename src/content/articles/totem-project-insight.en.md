Field notes from totem refurbishment work. Site details and client identities are
deliberately omitted.

## Conditions encountered

Totem refurbishment is generally more than a panel swap. At several sites the foundation
pole had corroded in areas not visible from the surface, making pole replacement part of the
scope of work.

## Lessons that repeat

**The initial survey defines the scope.** Sites surveyed thoroughly tend to finish faster,
because mid-project scope changes are the main cause of delay.

**The operating window must be realistic.** Setting a target date without accounting for the
facility's busy hours only moves the disruption rather than removing it.

**Document from the start.** Photos of the initial condition protect both parties when
questions arise about the existing state.
